🔐 NextStep Study — Privacy & Account Data

Updated November 26, 2026. A straightforward product notice, not a substitute for a complete legal privacy policy.

Guest use

If you use Guest Mode, JLPT level, language, study goals and some study progress are saved in your own browser storage. A verified account is not created. We cannot identify every guest or count them as registered users. You can clear site storage in your browser to remove local guest information.

Online accounts, when enabled

Google, email/password and optional Facebook sign-in require a configured Firebase project. Until the site owner enables the service, these buttons do not authenticate anyone. A consented, authenticated account stores its Firebase UID, display name, email, sign-in provider, account creation date, last sign-in date and last reported activity date in a private Firestore document. Passwords are handled by the auth provider and are not stored in study profiles.

Who can see personal details?

Firestore rules restrict each user's profile to the user and a specifically authorized administrator. Other learners cannot list or open your profile. The administrator can see registered accounts and recent recorded use dates to manage the service. Activity dates are client-reported and can be delayed; they are not proof of attendance.

Consent and choices

Online sign-in asks for consent to record private account and activity information. You can study as a Guest without creating an account. For account questions or deletion requests, use the Feedback Center; account deletion and data removal require administrator/provider handling and are not automatic from this notice. An approved site operator should provide verified contact and retention details before public account launch.

Services, limitations and external links

Translation may request text from an external translation provider if you choose that feature. Facebook, Google, and Japanese speech services may have separate rules. When enabled, phone sign-in sends the number to Google for authentication and abuse prevention. The study directory stores the account ID and provider. No third-party advertiser is granted access to the private account directory by these site features.
