# NextStep Japanese JLPT N2 — 3 Study Books + Chapter Practice

## Overview
- **Kanji Notebook** — 400 numbered study pages: **398 distinct N2-level-tagged characters** and **2 revision pages**, divided into 10 chapters of 40 pages.
- **Vocabulary Notebook** — 300 numbered study pages featuring **1,778 distinct N2-level-tagged words/expressions**, divided into 10 chapters of 30 pages.
- **Grammar Notebook** — 300 numbered study pages covering **123 N2-level-tagged grammar patterns**, arranged into 10 chapters of 30 pages with formation, examples, explanations, exercises and reviews.
- **Chapter Practice Papers** — **50 automatically assembled original four-choice questions per chapter**, with 10 chapters per book. **30 papers × 50 questions = 1,500 distinct question IDs**. Correct answers and detailed Japanese, English and Nepali explanations are revealed after grading.

## Getting started
Open `index.html` in a browser or use your GitHub Pages site: `https://dadalupendra.github.io/nextstep-study/N2/`. Select Kanji / Vocabulary / Grammar, choose Chapter 1–10, and tap **Chapter test · 50 Questions** when ready. You may also open `quiz.html?book=grammar&chapter=1` directly.

### Study features
- Large font-size choices (18 to 32 CSS pixels) and mobile-responsive design.
- N2-target kanji with radicals, stroke counts, on-/kun-readings, meaning and contextual N2 words where available.
- N2-target vocabulary with readings, English meaning, part of speech and example sentences.
- N2-target grammar with meanings in Nepali and English, formation rules, usage notes and multiple Japanese/English example sentences.
- Search, Japanese browser speech synthesis, progress tracking, bookmarks and personal notes (stored in browser localStorage).
- `print.html` supports browser Print → Save as PDF; actual PDF page count depends on browser/paper settings.
- Practice explanations in Japanese, English and Nepali. These are original **JLPT-style study questions**, **not** past official JLPT questions. Mostly recognition/usage practice; they do not replace authentic full-section reading or listening tests.

## Scope & important limitations
The JLPT does **not** publish exhaustive official vocabulary, kanji or grammar lists for current levels. All target words, characters and patterns in these three books are **tagged N2 in the independent OpenJLPT dataset**, rather than copied from N3/N4/N5 tags. Natural example sentences may contain simpler prerequisite Japanese.

The 400 kanji **pages** do **not** mean 400 different N2 kanji; the underlying source has **398**, and the last two pages are revision. Similarly, 300 grammar study **pages** do not imply 300 different grammar points; there are **123** plus follow-up and review pages.

**Translation coverage:** all 123 grammar meanings have Nepali summaries. Vocabulary meanings are sourced English glosses; the 1,778 word list does **not** have full per-entry Nepali translation. Japanese text and many sample sentences come from the licensed source; the study instructions and question explanations include Nepali.
