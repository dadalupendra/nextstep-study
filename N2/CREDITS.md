## Scope
- 398 N2 level-tagged kanji characters + two self-review pages = 400 kanji pages.
- 1,778 N2 level-tagged vocabulary terms = 300 vocabulary pages.
- 123 N2 level-tagged grammar items = 300 grammar pages.
- 10 chapters per subject × 50 JLPT-style practice items = 1,500 book-question instances.
- Additional practice modes reuse curated N2 source item questions and offer original workplace situations for BJT-inspired study. Different exams do not have interchangeable official levels/scores.

### Furigana
The original OpenJLPT vocabulary and grammar entries provide source-marked readings, including furigana for many word examples and grammar example sentences. This project displays those source-confirmed readings with a show/hide toggle; where the source contains no full reading, no unverified pronunciation is synthesized. Answers for reading quizzes remain masked before selection.

## Quality and limitations
JavaScript assembles some distractors programmatically. A uniquely marked correct answer is supported by target data, but a natural language sentence may sometimes allow an alternative expression; human teacher review remains recommended before formal teaching, publication or sale. These materials are practice only, not official mock papers, and no official test-style score is claimed.

The project does not guarantee a perfect N2-only boundary because the official JLPT does not publish comprehensive level-specific vocabulary/kanji/grammar inventories. Every **tested target** comes from a source entry tagged N2; surrounding grammar, particles, and simple words in example sentences naturally overlap earlier JLPT levels.

Editorially created Japanese-language question templates, multilingual teaching notes and business scenarios belong to this NextStep edition; redistribution of derived OpenJLPT material should follow the upstream CC BY-SA terms.
