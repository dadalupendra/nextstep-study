JLPT Practice Paper Collection

日本語で問題を解き、正解・不正解の理由を日本語・नेपाली・English で学ぶ。

⚠️ Independent practice, not an official JLPT paper.

20 automatically assembled practice papers with 100 questions each are now available per level (100 paper instances / 10,000 question placements). These are vocabulary and kanji-focused review papers, not full JLPT-format mock exams. Questions and source words recur across different papers; no listening recordings or full-length reading exercises are currently included. Opening a paper requires its level data to download once. After that, the PWA can cache the dataset for offline practice when supported.

These short samples are not equivalent to a real exam score. The JLPT does not fix an identical number of questions in every administration. This project intentionally uses 100 practice items per paper at your request, not an official JLPT question allocation.
