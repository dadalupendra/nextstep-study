Records marked N4 are the community-maintained study category from that
dataset. The official JLPT does not publish an exhaustive current list of
vocabulary or kanji assigned to each level. This app does not claim to reproduce
official JLPT questions or licensed textbooks.

Japanese examples and English translations are source-derived. Machine
translations in Nepali or other languages may need internet access and may
contain mistakes. Confirm language details against trustworthy references.
